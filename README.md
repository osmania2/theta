# theta
Theta [a] (uppercase Θ or Θ; lowercase θ; cursive θ) is the eighth letter of the Greek alphabet, derived from the Phoenician letter Teth 𐤈.
Theta (uppercase Θ, lowercase θ) is the eighth letter of the Greek alphabet and has primary meanings across several disciplines:
• Greek Alphabet & Language: The letter has a value of 9 in Greek numerals and represents the "th" sound (as in "think").
• Mathematics & Physics: Lowercase θ is used as a variable to represent an unknown or measured angle (such as in sine, cosine, and tangent functions), or angular position. Uppercase Θ is used in computer science for Big-Theta notation (asymptotic tight bounds).
• Finance & Options Trading: Theta is one of "the Greeks," measuring the rate of time decay—how much value an option loses each day as it approaches its expiration date.
• Blockchain & Technology: Theta Network is a Layer 1 decentralized blockchain and GPU cloud infrastructure designed for video streaming, AI compute, and digital entertainment, powered by its native tokens THETA and TFUEL.
